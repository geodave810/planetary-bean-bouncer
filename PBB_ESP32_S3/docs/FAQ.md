## FAQ

### Is this a beginner-friendly project?
No. This project is intended for experienced makers who are comfortable working with mechanical assemblies, electronics, and heat sources. It is shared as an experimental DIY design rather than a turnkey or beginner kit.

### Can I build this without the planetary gear or wobble disc?
Yes. The design is modular, and builders are encouraged to experiment. The wobble disc and planetary gear system are included to improve bean agitation, but alternative approaches are possible depending on your goals.

### What batch size does this support?
Batch size depends on the specific drum, motor, gearing, and configuration used. Small test batches are recommended during initial setup and tuning before attempting larger roasts.

### Is this food safe?
This is an experimental DIY project and is not food-safety certified. Components that contact the beans are designed to be made from stainless steel (typically 304 or 316L), which are commonly considered food-safe materials. However, no guarantees are made, and builders are responsible for material selection, cleanliness, and safe operation.

### Why are the electronics, firmware, and build instructions not included here?
To keep versioning and documentation manageable, firmware, electronics, and detailed build instructions are maintained in the GitHub repository rather than distributed with the STL files. Refer to the main project documentation before building.
